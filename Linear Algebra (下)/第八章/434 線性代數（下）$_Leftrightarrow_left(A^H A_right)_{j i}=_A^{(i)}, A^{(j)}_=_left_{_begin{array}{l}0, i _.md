434 線性代數（下）

$\Leftrightarrow\left(A^H A\right)_{j i}=<A^{(i)}, A^{(j)}>=\left\{\begin{array}{l}0, i \neq j \\ 1, i=j\end{array} \Leftrightarrow A^H A=I \Leftrightarrow A\right.$ 為么正矩陣．
$A$ 的列向量形成單範正交
$\Leftrightarrow\left(A A^H\right)_{i j}=<A_{(i)}, A_{(j)}>=\left\{\begin{array}{l}0, i \neq j \\ 1, i=j\end{array} \Leftrightarrow A A^H=I \Leftrightarrow A\right.$ 為么正矩陣．
例如，$D=\left[\begin{array}{cc}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{-1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right]$ ，則
$\because\left[\begin{array}{c}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{c}\frac{-1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right]>=0$ ，且 $\left\|\left[\begin{array}{c}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right]\right\|=1,\left\|\left[\begin{array}{c}\frac{-1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{array}\right]\right\|=1$,
即行向量為單範正交集，故 $D$ 為正交矩陣．
