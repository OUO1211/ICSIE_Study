$\because\langle T(\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{v}\rangle=0, \forall \boldsymbol{v} \in V$,
$\therefore\langle T(\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{v}\rangle=0,\langle T(\boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{u}\rangle=0,\langle T(\boldsymbol{u}+\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{u}+\boldsymbol{v}\rangle=0, \forall \boldsymbol{u}, \boldsymbol{v} \in V$ ，
又 $<T(\boldsymbol{u}+\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{u}+\boldsymbol{v}>=<T(\boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{u}>+<T(\boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{v}>+<T(\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{v}>+<T(\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{u}>$
故得 $\langle T(\boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{v}\rangle+\langle T(\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{u}\rangle=0, \ldots \boldsymbol{*}^{\boldsymbol{*}}$
同理可得，$\langle T(i \boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{v}\rangle+\langle T(\boldsymbol{v}), i \boldsymbol{u}\rangle=0$ ，
而 $\langle T(i \boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{v}\rangle+\langle T(\boldsymbol{v}), i \boldsymbol{u}\rangle=i\langle T(\boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{v}\rangle-i\langle T(\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{u}\rangle=0$ ，
即 $<T(\boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{v}>-<T(\boldsymbol{v}), \boldsymbol{u}>=0, \ldots \ldots \ldots .^{* *}$
由＊與＊＊兩式可得 $\langle T(\boldsymbol{u}), \boldsymbol{v}\rangle=0, \forall \boldsymbol{u}, \boldsymbol{v} \in V, \therefore$ 由（8）可知 $T$ 為零映射。
