$\mathrm{T}(\mathrm{n})=7 \mathrm{~T}\left(\frac{\mathrm{n}}{3}\right)+\mathrm{n}^2$
解 $\because \mathrm{a}=7, \mathrm{~b}=3, \mathrm{f}(\mathrm{n})=\mathrm{n}^2$
而 $\mathrm{n}^{\log _e x^{\prime}}=\mathrm{n}^{\log _e{ }^{\prime}}$
而 $\frac{\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{n})}{\mathrm{n}^{\log _6}{ }^{\circ}}=\frac{\mathrm{n}^2}{\mathrm{n}^{\log _6}{ }^7}=\mathrm{n}^{2-\log _3,}$
$\left(\because 2-\log _3 7>\phi\right.$ ，since $1<\log _3 7<2$ ）
∴ Apply case 3 of the master theorem，
$\Rightarrow \mathrm{T}(\mathrm{n})=\mathrm{n}^2$

例題 1－36
$\mathrm{T}(\mathrm{n})=2 \mathrm{~T}\left(\frac{\mathrm{n}}{4}\right)+\sqrt{\mathrm{n}}$
解 $\because \mathrm{a}=2, \mathrm{~b}=4, \mathrm{f}(\mathrm{n})=\sqrt{\mathrm{n}}$
and $n^{\log _b{ }^a}=n^{\log _4{ }^2}=\sqrt{n}$
而 $\frac{\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{n})}{\mathrm{n}^{\log _{\mathrm{b}} \mathrm{a}}}=\frac{\sqrt{\mathrm{n}}}{\sqrt{\mathrm{n}}}=1$
∴ Apply case 2 of master theorem
$\therefore \mathrm{T}(\mathrm{n})=\theta(\sqrt{\mathrm{n}} \log \mathrm{n})$
