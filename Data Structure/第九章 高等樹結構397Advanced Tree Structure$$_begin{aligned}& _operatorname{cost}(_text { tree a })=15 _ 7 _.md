$$
\begin{aligned}
& \operatorname{cost}(\text { tree a })=2.56 \\
& \operatorname{cost}(\text { tree b })=1.9 \\
& \operatorname{cost}(\text { tree c })=1.5 \\
& \operatorname{cost}(\text { tree d })=2.05 \\
& \operatorname{cost}(\text { tree e })=1.6
\end{aligned}
$$
則 c 是最佳樹。
